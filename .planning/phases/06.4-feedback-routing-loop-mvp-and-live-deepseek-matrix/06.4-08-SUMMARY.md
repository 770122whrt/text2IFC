## Implemented

- Failed Gate evidence is authoritative even when raw Audit prose says accept.
- Final acceptance rechecks the complete Gate summary and cannot be reached by
  compile/reopen plus geometry booleans alone.
- Dynamic opening/filling checks cover host-local bounds, placement chains,
  relative orientation, and filling/opening bounds with component IDs.
- Gate and Audit details are retained in Generator feedback instead of being
  collapsed to one generic failure sentence.
- Non-improvement compares structured issue evidence. Equal issue counts do
  not stop a loop when affected components or actual/expected evidence changed.
- Feedback rounds and evaluation evidence are append-only and numbered.
- Missing storey height or slab thickness is unresolved, not silently replaced
  with 3000/150 defaults.
- Storey ordering supports arbitrary numeric storeys and explicit elevations.
- Scaffold IFC remains available for diagnosis but is never live acceptance.
- Candidate provenance distinguishes initial live generation, live
  regeneration, and deterministic Scaffold fallback.
- The controlled two-storey input now fixes the first-floor slab datum and the
  stair footprint/run facts required for a non-invented live Design Brief.
