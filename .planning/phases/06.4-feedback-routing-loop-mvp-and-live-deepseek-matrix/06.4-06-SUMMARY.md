accepted/non-accept runs. The remaining review gap was that a human reviewer
had to inspect many sidecars to answer one question:

> Are the important workflow links actually backed by real DeepSeek evidence

Wave 6 adds a single coverage matrix for that question. It does not create
Phase 6.5 and does not claim complex multi-storey generation is solved.

complex building prompt will produce an accepted IFC, and it does not claim
