## Non-blocking Boundaries

- v1.0 ships the supported IFC2X3 semantic-generation profile; it does not
  claim general material, mapped geometry, BRep, tessellation, structural,
  furnishing, or MEP generation.
- The successful live stability evidence uses adaptive/automated drivers.
  The true-human REPL implementation exists, but its final successful UAT is
  deferred and must be reintroduced as an explicit requirement if it is part
  of a future milestone.
- Three-case stability is coverage-oriented, not a repeated-run probability.
