<objective>
Call real Mimo repair only for eligible deterministic failures and prove the
repair did not invent facts or conceal unresolved ambiguity.
</objective>

<why>
Repair is useful for contract mistakes derivable from known evidence. It is
dangerous when used to fill missing user semantics. The aborted attempt-05
demonstrated that supervisor-authored reinterpretation must be excluded.
</why>

<must_haves>
- First-pass success records `no_repair_needed` and zero repair calls.
- Repair eligibility is an explicit deterministic issue-class policy.
- One repair attempt maximum.
- Repair receives original evidence and exact diagnostics, not supervisor facts.
- Before/after issues and semantic fact delta are persisted.
- Need for a new user fact routes canonical Draft/clarification.
</must_haves>
