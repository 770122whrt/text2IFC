<objective>
Build the transcript-preserving loop that reruns the real Design Brief Agent
after user answers and stops only at ready, canonical Draft, or blocked status.
</objective>

<why>
Phase 5 has deterministic question planning and merge logic, while the new live
architecture assigns semantic interpretation and question text to Design Brief
Agent v2. The controller must enforce structure without becoming another Agent.
</why>

<must_haves>
- Original Chinese bytes and every turn remain immutable.
- One to three model-authored questions are presented in order.
- Answers are appended, not merged by hand into semantic JSON.
- Every answer causes a new real Mimo Design Brief call with full history.
- Unknown answers remain explicit and cannot produce defaults.
- The live pipeline does not call `plan_questions`.
</must_haves>
