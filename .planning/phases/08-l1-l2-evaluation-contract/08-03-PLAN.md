<objective>
Convert current preservation and Window geometry evidence into an independent,
structured L1 decision that measures the IFC itself and proves every actual
effect is permitted by both policy and the declared ChangeSet intent.
</objective>

<threat_model>
- T-08-03A (high): a faulty Applicator lists its own collateral edits as
  allowed, causing preservation to pass. Mitigation: independent actual IFC
  diff cross-checked against Registry policy and ChangeSet scope.
- T-08-03B (high): duplicate or extra IFC relationships preserve visually
  plausible geometry while corrupting topology. Mitigation: exact chain
  cardinality/endpoint checks and unexplained-relation detection.
- T-08-03C (medium): tolerance or representation ordering hides geometry drift.
  Mitigation: normalized GlobalId/role measurements with versioned tolerances,
  never STEP-order comparison.
</threat_model>
