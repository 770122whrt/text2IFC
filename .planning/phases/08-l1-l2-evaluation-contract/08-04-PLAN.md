<threat_model>
- T-08-04A (critical): private original values, IDs, paths, or mutation mapping
  leak into Provider/public artifacts. Mitigation: separate input types,
  evaluator-only entrypoint, allowlist projection, and full-bundle canary scan.
- T-08-04B (high): an L1-passing/L2-failing IFC is labeled or published as a
  completed repair. Mitigation: strict terminal/publication status derived only
  from Evaluation 0.2.
- T-08-04C (medium): benchmark comparison requires original GUID/STEP identity
  and falsely fails recreated roles. Mitigation: private mutation role mapping
  plus semantic/relationship equivalence.
</threat_model>
