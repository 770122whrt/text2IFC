Apply bound Window semantic assignments to IFC2X3 transactionally, reopen and
independently extract them, and close Production/benchmark L2 without weakening
the proven L1 geometry and preservation path.
</objective>

<threat_model>
- T-10-13 (critical): a partial Pset/association write survives a later failure. Mitigation: semantic authoring runs within the existing temporary-copy transaction and publishes only after reopen/L1/L2.
- T-10-14 (critical): evaluator trusts applicator self-report. Mitigation: reopen and independently extract every actual semantic fact.
- T-10-15 (high): Material/Classification reuse mutates unrelated shared relationships. Mitigation: reuse resources through new deterministic association relationships scoped to the created Window.
- T-10-16 (high): Type Psets are copied as occurrence facts and drift. Mitigation: formal Type binding plus ownership-aware skip.
</threat_model>
