<objective>
Build the complete per-operation Window semantic authoring manifest from
request/current-IFC/authorized-Type evidence, including safe Material and
Classification reuse, without Gold, RAG, arbitrary-neighbor copying, or
occurrence-to-Type promotion.
</objective>

<threat_model>
- T-10-05 (critical): a private original or mutation mapping influences production authoring. Mitigation: signatures exclude private inputs and canary-scan every public artifact.
- T-10-06 (critical): same-Type occurrence voting promotes Level/Host/identity into Type authority. Mitigation: separate cohort source kind, operation-specific allowlist, and direct-vs-inherited tests.
- T-10-07 (high): one conflicting cohort value is silently selected. Mitigation: canonical set comparison and fail-closed conflict result.
- T-10-08 (high): missing Material is treated as present or vice versa. Mitigation: explicit `authorized`, `verified_absent`, and `conflict` applicability states.
</threat_model>
