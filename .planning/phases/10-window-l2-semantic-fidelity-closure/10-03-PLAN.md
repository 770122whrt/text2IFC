<objective>
Replace the geometry-only Provider-to-application handoff with a compact,
hash-bound Stage 2 draft and a deterministic self-contained executable
ChangeSet 0.2, without increasing Provider semantic payload or weakening Audit.
</objective>

<threat_model>
- T-10-09 (critical): Stage 2 invents or alters a semantic value. Mitigation: draft carries only refs/explicit slot refs and Binder copies values from the immutable manifest.
- T-10-10 (critical): a stale manifest is rebound to another IFC/request/operation. Mitigation: fingerprint, request hash, operation ID/type, policy version, and manifest hash checks.
- T-10-11 (high): full semantic or private evidence leaks into Provider prompt/trace. Mitigation: allowlisted prompt projection and whole-trace canary tests.
- T-10-12 (high): historical ChangeSet 0.1 is silently executed as 0.2. Mitigation: exact-version parsing and explicit compatibility/upgrade behavior.
</threat_model>
