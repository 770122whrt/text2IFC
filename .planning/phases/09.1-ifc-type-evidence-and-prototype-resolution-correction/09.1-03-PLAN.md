<objective>
Build formal/user-approved Type semantic evidence directly from TypeRecord,
eliminate occurrence-derived false conflicts, and keep genuine conflicts and
missing L2 authority fail-closed.
</objective>

<threat_model>
- T-09.1-09 (critical): occurrence facts masquerade as approved Prototype Type
  facts. Mitigation: required TypeRecord mapping and no occurrence aggregation
  on the v0.2 path.
- T-09.1-10 (critical): fixing the conflict weakens L2 or publishes a false
  success. Mitigation: unchanged policy/evaluator and terminal-matrix tests.
- T-09.1-11 (high): unauthorized or private Type records enter production.
  Mitigation: closed semantic kinds/source allowlist and Gold-canary signature
  tests.
- T-09.1-12 (high): a genuine contradictory Type authority is silently chosen.
  Mitigation: deterministic conflict detection remains fail-closed.
</threat_model>
