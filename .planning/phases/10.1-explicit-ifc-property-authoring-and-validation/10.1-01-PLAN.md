<objective>
Freeze a versioned Stage 1 exact-property claim and deterministic no-RAG
standard/custom resolver before adding confirmation or IFC mutation behavior.
</objective>

<threat_model>
- T-10.1-01 (critical): Stage 1 marks an invented property as standard/authorized. Mitigation: schema contains claim fields only; deterministic resolver owns classification.
- T-10.1-02 (high): a typo is silently corrected into another standard property. Mitigation: case-sensitive exact lookup; near matches remain unselected diagnostics.
- T-10.1-03 (high): generated IFC2X3 knowledge drift changes type/applicability. Mitigation: `check_registry_files()` before resolution and stable drift failure.
- T-10.1-04 (high): an ambiguous decimal is authored with the wrong measure. Mitigation: unresolved custom decimal/measure requires explicit type/unit clarification.
- T-10.1-05 (high): RepairIntent 0.1 evidence is reinterpreted as 0.2. Mitigation: exact version schemas/models and frozen compatibility fixtures.
</threat_model>
