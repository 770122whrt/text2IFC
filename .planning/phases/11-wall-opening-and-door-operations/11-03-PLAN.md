<threat_model>

- **Copied behavior drift:** Characterize Window behavior before extraction and
  run all Window application/batch regressions after every shared-core change.
- **Shared-Type mutation:** Exact Type reuse may add the new occurrence to an
  `IfcRelDefinesByType`, but the Type object, maps, Psets, materials and formal
  attributes must fingerprint identically before/after.
- **Template tampering:** Generated factory accepts only binder-owned,
  digest-verified derivation and enumerated formal values.
- **Geometric collision:** Every hosted-opening operation exposes one
  canonical wall-local footprint and participates in cross-family checks.
- **Partial publication:** All construction occurs in memory/temporary output;
  audit/application/postcondition/reopen failures publish nothing.
- **Semantic invention:** Simplified visual geometry parameters are compiler
  policy and are not emitted as user-authorized construction facts.

</threat_model>
