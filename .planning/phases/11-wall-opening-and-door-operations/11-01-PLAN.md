<threat_model>

- **Profile injection:** Profile IDs, contracts and few-shots are checked-in,
  schema-validated and hash-bound. A Provider cannot register or alter a
  capability.
- **Private evidence leakage:** Retain all private canaries. Profiles and
  examples contain sentinels only; no original IFC, mutation manifest or
  benchmark Gold may enter either Provider payload.
- **Authority confusion:** Stage 2 still emits a non-authoritative draft.
  Semantic assignments and generated Type derivation are injected only by the
  deterministic binder.
- **Token amplification:** Bound profile/few-shot counts, individual bytes and
  union size. Stage 2 receives only selected profiles in deterministic order.
- **Historical contract drift:** Never edit 0.1–0.4 intent, 0.1/0.2 manifest or
  0.1–0.3 ChangeSet schema bytes.

</threat_model>
