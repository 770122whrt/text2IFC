<objective>
Connect RepairIntent to the Phase 7 index/resolver/context boundary and then to
a revised ChangeSet Agent that operates only on fully resolved, bounded public
authority, producing one completely bound unified ChangeSet or a typed
non-application state.
</objective>

<threat_model>
- T-09-03A (critical): Stage 2 substitutes an out-of-context target, scope, or
  evidence pointer. Mitigation: one-to-one deterministic binding validation
  against fingerprint-bound contexts before Audit.
- T-09-03B (high): stale index/context is reused after source change. Mitigation:
  exact source/index/extractor/schema/model fingerprint validation at every
  transition and resume.
- T-09-03C (high): one invalid operation is dropped and the remaining subset is
  applied. Mitigation: cardinality, operation-ID set equality, and unified
  ChangeSet rejection before any side effect.
</threat_model>
