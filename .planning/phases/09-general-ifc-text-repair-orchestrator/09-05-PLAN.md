<objective>
Expose the general orchestrator through one thin interactive/machine CLI and
finish Phase 9 with deterministic offline state-matrix and LargeBuilding
evidence, full regression/security checks, and one clearly opt-in real
DeepSeek two-stage UAT route whose actual L1/L2 outcome is reported honestly.
</objective>

<threat_model>
- T-09-05A (high): CLI rendering leaks full prompts, private facts, credentials,
  or huge payloads. Mitigation: typed allowlisted renderers, redaction,
  artifact references, bounded stdout, and stdout/stderr tests.
- T-09-05B (high): interactive EOF/cancel or non-interactive ambiguity falls
  through to unintended target selection. Mitigation: explicit answer/cancel
  parsing and identical clarification state returned by the Python API.
- T-09-05C (medium): live network evidence is mistaken for deterministic
  acceptance or current Window L2 failure is mislabeled success. Mitigation:
  opt-in marker/config gate, separate report section, exact call counts, and
  Evaluation 0.2 truth preserved.
</threat_model>
