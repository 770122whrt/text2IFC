<threat_model>

- **Cache poisoning/staleness:** Verify the full five-part cache key, payload
  hash, complete-write marker, bounded schema, and atomic rename before reuse.
  Corrupt or mismatched entries are misses.
- **Parallel false pass:** Each worker reopens its own IFC path; no mutable
  IfcOpenShell object crosses workers. Any exception, timeout, missing result,
  or sequential/accelerated disagreement blocks publication.
- **Memory exhaustion:** Default to at most two workers and record process-tree
  peak RSS. Exceeding 4 GiB terminates the accelerated path as failed evidence.
- **Provider fallback:** Live UAT records provider/model/attempts and may end
  honestly `provider_failed`; it must not substitute offline intent/ChangeSet
  while reporting a live success.

</threat_model>
