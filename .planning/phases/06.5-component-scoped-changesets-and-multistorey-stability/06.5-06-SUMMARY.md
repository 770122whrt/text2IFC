acceptance boundary required for later graded UAT. Its original requirement of
three consecutive accepted two-storey runs plus three consecutive accepted
three-storey runs was not completed and is not claimed.

The repeated-run gate was subsequently superseded by the coverage-oriented
Easy/Medium/Hard policy recorded in Waves 7 and 8. This summary closes the
historical documentation gap; it does not create new live evidence.

  independent sessions, acceptance accounting, and the absence of scripted
