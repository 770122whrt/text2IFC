# Phase 6.5-04: Gate/Audit Revision Evidence, Reports, and Timing

**Goal:** Make every revision auditable while keeping local correction fast and final acceptance strict.

**Architecture:** Run component-relevant local Gates immediately after a patch,
then mandatory global schema/semantic/relationship/compile/reopen/geometry Gates
before Audit. Reports summarize revisions from trace artifacts and the session
DB; they do not duplicate or invent execution state.
