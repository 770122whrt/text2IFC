## Medium Risks to Monitor During Execution

1. Scope derivation depends on Issues carrying stable target identity. Wave 1
   must block unresolved identity and may require targeted enrichment of Gate
   evidence rather than fuzzy name matching.
2. `repair_json` and ChangeSet-based `regenerate_json` must remain distinct.
   A syntax repair must not become an alternate unrestricted model editor.
3. Staged generation increases provider call count. Wave 4 timing evidence and
   compact traces must measure the trade-off before any performance claim.
4. The six accepted live sessions are a stability threshold, not statistical
   proof of generalization. Reports must state that limitation.

## User-review Decisions

The plan is executable without another product decision. The user may still
choose to change two deliberately strict policies before execution:

- require fewer or more than three consecutive live successes per case;
- defer staged initial generation and ship scoped feedback repair alone.

The current recommendation is to keep both policies as written.
