## Purpose

This inventory preserves the useful conclusions from local Hard live/debug
runs before the C-drive worktree is removed. The canonical successful evidence
is committed under
`dataset/processed/agent-demo/phase6.5-hard-accepted/`, including a compressed
copy of the complete accepted trace. The raw directories below total 1,450
files and approximately 94.09 MB, mostly repeated prompts, responses, SQLite
databases, intermediate candidates, and compiled IFC attempts.

They are deliberately not committed wholesale. A generated IFC in a blocked or
Draft run is not acceptance evidence.
