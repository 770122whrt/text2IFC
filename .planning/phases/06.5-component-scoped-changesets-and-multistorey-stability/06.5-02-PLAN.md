# Phase 6.5-02: ChangeSet Generator and Feedback-loop Integration

**Goal:** Replace full-document `regenerate_json` execution with a real provider-backed scoped ChangeSet round.

**Architecture:** Preserve Phase 6.4 Issue normalization and routing. When the
route owner is Generator, derive scope deterministically, render a dedicated
ChangeSet prompt, validate/apply the response, then feed the complete composed
candidate into existing semantic, Gate, compiler, geometry, and Audit stages.
