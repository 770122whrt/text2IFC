# Phase 6.5 Research

## Finding 1: The local defect is replacement, not missing feedback

Phase 6.4 already creates evidence-rich `regenerate_json` feedback. The current
implementation then calls the normal Generator contract and promotes its full
candidate. Prompt instructions cannot guarantee preservation of unrelated
objects. A deterministic merge boundary is therefore required.

## Finding 2: Raw RFC 6902-style array patches are a poor public contract

BIM JSON stores entity and relationship collections. Array-index paths are
unstable when the model reorders, adds, or removes records. Phase 6.5 should
resolve operations by stable semantic ID, then use ordinary structured Python
updates internally. Field paths remain useful inside one identified record.

## Finding 3: IFC5 is useful as a design reference, not a dependency

buildingSMART's IFC5 development material explores object/component
composition, data distributed across layers/files, and overriding information
without destroying the original dataset. Earlier technical direction also
identifies modularization and incremental updates as goals. The repository is
alpha/development material, so production adoption would add unnecessary risk.

Primary references:

- https://github.com/buildingSMART/IFC5-development
- https://github.com/buildingSMART/IFC5-development/blob/main/Examples_FAQ.md
- https://ifc5.technical.buildingsmart.org/
- https://www.buildingsmart.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/IFC_5.pdf

## Finding 4: One mechanism can serve repair and staged generation

A ChangeSet applicator can update a complete revision during feedback and can
also compose declared packages into an internal workspace during initial
generation. Reusing one merge and scope engine avoids two competing notions of
model mutation. Only the final composition is validated and promoted as Formal
BIM JSON 2.0.

## Rejected Approaches

1. **Prompt-only freeze list**: cheap, but Phase 6.4 already demonstrated that
   unrelated content can drift despite preservation instructions.
2. **Continue full-document regeneration plus post-hoc diff**: detects damage
   after paying for a large call and leaves ambiguous merge choices.
3. **Direct IFC entity mutation**: bypasses BIM JSON as structural truth and
   makes provenance and deterministic validation harder.
4. **Adopt IFC5/IFCX now**: outside the IFC2X3 target and dependent on an alpha
   specification.

## Recommended Architecture

- Versioned ID-addressed ChangeSet sidecar.
- Candidate index and per-component semantic hashes.
- Deterministic scope/dependency planner.
- Immutable revision applicator.
- Separate ChangeSet Generator prompt and provider trace.
- Relevant local Gates followed by mandatory full acceptance Gates.
- Same engine for staged skeleton/storey/cross-storey composition.

## Risks Requiring Execution Evidence

- A Generator may fail to express a correction as a small ChangeSet even when
  it can regenerate a whole document.
- Dependency closure may be too narrow for coupled opening/host/stair/slab
  failures or too broad to provide meaningful preservation guarantees.
- Three consecutive live acceptances may expose model variance in the first
  candidate that scoped repair cannot recover within three rounds.
- Staged composition may reduce per-call complexity but increase cross-package
  consistency failures. Hash-bound ownership and global verification are
  therefore mandatory.
