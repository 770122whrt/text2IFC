   rather than incorrectly authorizing only the related opening ID.
2. Contract and scope retries share a bounded three-attempt ChangeSet budget,
   so one contract correction does not consume the entire application budget.
3. Opening Gate feedback now exposes the canonical local-frame axes as
   machine-readable evidence:

   `Representation.direction=[0,0,1]`.
4. No fallback was added that copies polygon points, rewrites coordinates, or
   weakens a Gate. Geometry remains Generator-owned and evidence-routed.
