## Follow-up closure — 2026-07-25

The 2026-07-24 Production replay above is retained as historical diagnostic
evidence. A minimal follow-up established that its two remaining failure
classes were evaluator/evidence-model false negatives:

- a user-approved mapped Window frame may be centred and contained inside its
  opening while retaining the requested nominal width and height;
- occurrence-direct material associations take precedence over bound-Type
  materials, while Type material remains effective when no direct association
  exists.

ChangeSet edit. The regenerated damaged model had the same source SHA-256 as
the original live transition. The complete replay finished in approximately
