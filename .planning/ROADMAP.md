## Candidate Next Scope

- Repeated-run statistical reliability for Easy/Medium/Difficult cases.
- Final successful true-human Chinese REPL acceptance (`CLI-08`).
- Broader IFC2X3 material, topology, arbitrary representation, and class
  generation (`GEO-03`, `GEO-04`, `GEO-05`, `IFC-06`).
- Generalized spatial collision checks and double-leaf door semantics.

These are candidates, not committed requirements for the next milestone.
